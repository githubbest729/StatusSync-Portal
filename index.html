            Install StatusSync Portal

            Install for a home-screen icon, a full-screen app window and offline access to your projects.

            • iPhone / iPad (Safari)

              1. Tap the Share button in the Safari toolbar.

              2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

              3. Tap Add. StatusSync appears on your Home Screen and opens full-screen.

            • Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

              1. Click the Install icon at the right end of the address bar.

              2. No icon? Open the browser menu (⋮) and choose Install StatusSync…

              3. Confirm Install, then pin the app to your taskbar or Dock.

            • Android (Chrome)

              1. Open the Chrome menu ⋮.

              2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).

              3. Confirm. The app opens without browser bars and works offline.
